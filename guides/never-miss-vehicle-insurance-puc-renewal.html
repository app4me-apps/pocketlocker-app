How to never miss car insurance, PUC and licence renewal dates

Updated 27 September 2026 · Pocket Locker Guides

Quick answer Write down every vehicle document with its expiry date in one place, and set a reminder 30 days and again 7 days before each date. Renew PUC and insurance before the last day — there is no grace period for PUC, and an expired insurance policy leaves you uninsured on the road.

Most people remember their vehicle papers only when a traffic police officer asks for them — or when a renewal SMS arrives after the date has already passed. Insurance, PUC and your driving licence all expire on different dates, and each one comes with a fine or a real risk if it lapses.

Why missing a vehicle renewal is costly

DocumentHow often it expiresWhat happens if it lapses
PUC certificate1 year for a new vehicle; after that usually 6–12 months depending on the vehicle's emission normFine of up to ₹10,000 under Section 190(2) of the Motor Vehicles Act in many states. No grace period.
Motor insuranceUsually every year (third-party can be multi-year)Driving uninsured is an offence, and any accident cost falls on you. A long break can also cost you your No Claim Bonus.
Driving licenceMany years, varies by age and licence typeDriving on an expired licence can lead to a fine and trouble with insurance claims.
Vehicle service / warrantyBy date or kilometresA missed service can affect your warranty cover.

A simple system that works

  1. Collect every document once. RC, insurance policy, latest PUC, driving licence, and the last service record — for every vehicle in the family.
  2. Note the exact expiry date of each. It's printed on the document: "valid up to" on the PUC, "policy period" on the insurance.
  3. Set two reminders per document: one 30 days before (time to compare insurance quotes) and one 7 days before (last call).
  4. Keep one folder per vehicle. "My Car", "Dad's Bike" — so you're never mixing up two policies.
  5. Update the date the day you renew. Otherwise next year's reminder never fires.
Showing documents to traffic police: the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways has said that a driving licence and RC shown through the government's DigiLocker or mParivahan apps are valid. A photo of your documents in any other app — including Pocket Locker — is for your own reference and reminders, not a replacement for those official apps.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a PUC certificate valid?

For a new vehicle the first PUC is valid for one year. After that it is usually valid for 6 to 12 months depending on the vehicle and its emission norm (BS-IV / BS-VI vehicles typically get 12 months). The exact validity is printed on your certificate.

What is the fine for driving without a valid PUC?

Under Section 190(2) of the Motor Vehicles Act, the fine can be up to ₹10,000 in many states. Amounts vary by state, so check your state transport department.

Is a photo of my driving licence on my phone valid?

Only documents shown through DigiLocker or mParivahan are officially recognised for traffic checks. Keep a photo in Pocket Locker for reference and reminders, and use DigiLocker or mParivahan (or the original) when an officer asks.

When should I renew my car insurance?

Before the policy end date. Starting about 30 days early gives you time to compare quotes and keep your No Claim Bonus without a gap in cover.
