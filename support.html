Getting started

What is Pocket Locker?

A private vault for your passwords, IDs, vehicle papers, insurance, bank details, warranties, certificates and notes. Everything is stored on your phone, sensitive details are encrypted, and nothing is sent to any server unless you choose to turn on Google Drive backup.

Do I need an account?

No. Pocket Locker works fully offline with no sign-up. You only connect a Google account if you choose to turn on Drive backup.

What categories are included?

Passwords, Notes, Vehicle, Identity, Warranty, Certificates and Bank — each with its own form (for example IFSC and UPI ID for Bank, username and website for Passwords). On Premium you can also create your own categories.

How do I add my first document?

Tap + on the home screen, choose a category, then choose a folder — or tap New Folder to create one (for example “My Car” or “HDFC Bank”). Fill in the details, add a photo, scan or PDF, and tap Save.

What's on the home screen?

Your plan (Free or Premium) and, on Free, how many of your 15 documents you've used. Below that are the search bar, the Sync button, Scan Document and Auto Fill shortcuts, Expiring Soon, your categories and your recent documents.

What are the tabs at the bottom?

Vault (home), Generator (password generator), the raised + to add a document from anywhere, Reminders, and More for settings, backup, Trash and help.

What kinds of entries can I save?

Five: Details (documents like RC, insurance or ID), Login, Note, Bank Account and Gallery for private photos. Each has its own form. Gallery is part of Premium.

Folders

Why do I need to create a folder first?

Every document lives inside a folder, so things stay organised as your vault grows — one folder per car, bank account or family member. Open a category and tap Add Folder, or create one while adding a document.

I updated the app and my old documents are in a “General” folder.

That's expected. Documents you saved before folders were introduced were moved into a General folder in the same category, with all their photos, PDFs and reminders. Nothing was deleted.

Can I change the icon of a folder or document?

Yes. Use the category's icon, pick an emoji or a brand icon, or choose a photo from your gallery.

How do I move a document to another folder or category?

Open the document and tap Move, then choose the new folder or category.

Can I rename the field names?

Yes, for Vehicle, Identity, Warranty and Certificate. Tap the pencil icon next to any of the first three fields to rename it for that one entry — for example “Title” to “Policy Name”. Password, Notes and Bank keep fixed field names.

How do I create my own category?

Tap Add New in Categories, give it a name (for example Insurance, Medical or Property), choose an icon (emoji, brand icon or photo) and pick the form it uses: Details, Password / Login, Note, Bank Account or Gallery (a Private Gallery for photos). Custom categories are part of Premium.

What are Quick Fill Types?

When adding a document, tap a type like RC Book or Insurance to fill the title in one tap. Tap New Type to add your own.

How do I keep important documents at the top?

Tap the star next to a document's name. Starred documents stay at the top of their lists.

Scanner & auto-fill

How does the document scanner work?

On the Add screen, tap Scan Document. The page edges are detected automatically and the page is straightened and cleaned. Scan several pages and they're saved together as one PDF. It's free on both plans.

What is auto-fill?

Tap Auto-fill from photo and take or choose a photo of your document. Pocket Locker reads numbers, names and dates and suggests which field each one goes into. You can edit or skip every value — nothing is filled until you tap Fill Fields.

Auto-fill picked up a wrong value.

Auto-fill reads printed text, so small, blurry or angled photos can confuse it. Use a clear, well-lit, straight photo (the scanner helps), and always check the values before saving.

Are my scans sent anywhere?

No. Scanning and auto-fill happen on your phone. Your images and the text read from them are not sent to us. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I crop a photo?

Yes. After taking or choosing a photo, a crop screen opens so you can keep just the document.

What does a scan save as — photo or PDF?

One page is saved as a photo. Two or more pages are saved together as one PDF.

How do I scan both sides of an ID card?

Scan the front and then the back. Pocket Locker places them at real card size, front on the left and back on the right, on one A4 page — just like a photocopy.

Can I auto-fill from a photo I already have?

Yes. Tap Auto Fill and choose Scan, Camera or Gallery. You check every value before it's filled in.

Can auto-fill read a PDF?

Yes. Auto-fill now works with PDFs too — for example an e-policy or e-ticket that came by email. As with photos, you check every value before it's filled in.

How do I save a file from WhatsApp, email or Files?

Open the photo or PDF, tap Share and choose Pocket Locker (on iPhone: Save to Pocket Locker). You can send up to 5 files at once, then pick where to save them.

Reminders

How do I get reminded before something expires?

Add the expiry date and turn on Remind me. Choose 30 or 7 days before. The home screen also shows everything Expiring Soon.

What's different on Premium?

Free lets you pick one reminder per document. Premium lets you set several (for example both 30 and 7 days before) and add a custom date and time. Reminders for Notes use a custom date and time, so they're a Premium feature.

I renewed my insurance — how do I update it?

Open the document and tap Renew. Enter the new expiry date (and a new photo if you like). The old date is kept in the document's renewal history, and reminders are updated for the new date.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for Pocket Locker in your phone's Settings, and that Remind me is on for that document. On Android, also allow “Alarms & reminders” and turn off battery optimisation for Pocket Locker if your phone offers it.

What time do reminders arrive?

Expiry reminders arrive at 9:00 AM. A custom reminder arrives at the exact date and time you pick.

How do the 7-day and 30-day options work?

7 days before reminds you every day at 9:00 AM from a week before the expiry date until the expiry day, then stops. 30 days before adds one reminder a month ahead, then the same daily reminders from 7 days before.

Why are the 7 and 30 day options greyed out?

They need an expiry or renewal date on the document. Add the date and the options become available.

Where can I see all my upcoming reminders?

Open the Reminders tab. Documents are grouped into Next 30 days, Expired and Later, and custom reminders appear as their own rows with their time. Tap a notification to open that document.

Can I be reminded to change a password or about a card expiry?

Yes. Add a Change password by date to a login, or a Card expiry date to a bank card, and turn on Remind me.

How do I check that notifications are working?

Go to More → Notifications. You can turn notifications on, send a test notification and see the next reminder your phone will show.

How do I stop reminders for a document?

Turn Remind me off on that document. If you change the date instead, reminders are created again for the new date.

What do reminder notifications say?

The title shows the document and its category — for example “Car insurance · Vehicle” — and the message says what's due. Tap it to open the document.

What is the Saturday reminder?

On Saturdays at 8:00 PM, Pocket Locker sends a gentle nudge to spend two minutes saving one more document. Turn it on or off in More → Notifications.

Using the app

How do I copy details quickly?

Tap the copy icon next to any value. To copy several at once, tap Copy All, tick the fields you want, and they're copied together with their labels — ready to paste into a message or form.

Can I use Light or Dark mode?

Yes. Go to Settings → Appearance and choose System, Light or Dark. System follows your phone's setting.

I deleted something by mistake — can I get it back?

Yes. Deleted items go to Settings → Trash and can be restored for 30 days. On the Free plan you can restore as long as you're under 15 documents.

Can I attach a photo or PDF?

Yes — take a photo, scan, choose from your gallery, or attach a PDF (up to 20 MB each), so the details and the actual document live together.

How does search work?

Tap the search bar to open full-page search. Best matches come first — title, then provider, number, category, folder and note text. Passwords are never searched.

Where can I see all my documents?

Tap View All next to Recent Documents. You can filter by category or see everything expiring in the next 30 days.

Can I see when a document was added or changed?

Yes. Open the document and look at Document Info — it shows when it was added and last changed.

Can I save an attachment to my phone?

Yes. Open the photo full screen and save a copy to your phone's Photos. PDFs open inside Pocket Locker with pinch-to-zoom; on Android, password-locked PDFs open in your phone's PDF app.

What is My Files?

A tab in All Documents that shows every photo and PDF you've saved in one grid. Filter by Photos, PDFs or category, and search by document name — handy when you don't remember where you saved something.

Can I open PDFs inside the app?

Yes. PDFs open inside Pocket Locker with pinch-to-zoom. On Android, password-locked PDFs open in your phone's PDF app instead.

How do I sort or filter All Documents?

Use the category chips at the top, sort by recently added, expiry date, title A–Z or amount, and filter by expiring in 30 days, expired or no expiry date.

Password Generator

How do I create a strong password?

Open the Generator tab, or tap the key button beside any Password field. Choose a length from 6 to 40 characters and whether to use upper case, lower case, numbers and symbols. A strength meter shows how strong it is.

What does “Avoid look-alike characters” do?

It leaves out characters that are easy to confuse, such as 0 and O or 1 and l, so the password is easier to read and type.

Does the generator keep a history?

Yes. It keeps your last 50 copied or used passwords, encrypted on your phone. You can delete one or clear them all.

Is a copied password cleared from the clipboard?

On Android, yes — a copied password is cleared after 60 seconds. On iPhone it isn't cleared automatically.

What does “Show large” do?

It shows a password in big numbered boxes, in groups of 4, with digits and symbols in colour — so it's easy to type on a laptop or TV without mistakes.

Notes

What can I do in the notes editor?

Notes open in a full-page editor with checklists, numbered and bullet lists, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, text and highlight colour, indent, divider lines, undo and redo. The Date button inserts today's date.

Are there typing shortcuts?

Yes. Type 1. to start a numbered list, - for a bullet list and [] for a checklist. Ticked checklist items move to the bottom.

Will I lose my note if the app locks while I'm writing?

No. Your draft is kept, so you can carry on where you left off.

Can I add a notes shortcut to my home screen?

Yes. Press and hold the app icon for New Note and My Notes. On Android you can drag New Note onto your home screen; on iPhone use the Shortcuts app (steps are in More → Guide).

Can I set a reminder on a note?

Yes, with Premium. Notes use a custom date and time reminder.

Private Gallery

What is Private Gallery?

A Premium feature that keeps private photos out of your phone's gallery and inside the locked vault. You organise them in albums, like Family or Private, and can add an extra 4-digit PIN.

How do I set up a Private Gallery?

Tap Add New in Categories, choose the 🔒 Gallery type, give it a name and create it. Inside, add albums such as Family or Private.

How do I lock photos?

Open an album and tap + to pick up to 30 photos — they're copied into Pocket Locker. You can also select photos in any app, tap Share › Pocket Locker and choose the album on the Lock Photos page; there's no form to fill.

Can I remove the photos from my phone's gallery?

Yes. After locking, Pocket Locker asks “Remove from your gallery?” and your phone asks you to confirm. On Android they're deleted for good (not moved to the Bin); on iPhone they stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Photos already backed up to Google Photos or iCloud stay there — the app tells you before removing.

Will locked photos show up elsewhere in the app?

No. Private is on by default, so locked photos don't appear in Recent Documents, Expiring Soon, All Documents, My Files or Search — nobody stumbles on them while you show another document.

How does the Private Gallery PIN work?

You set one 4-digit PIN for all your locked Private Gallery categories; a blue lock shows on the category. It asks for the PIN even when the app is already open, and locks again when you leave or after 15 seconds away.

I forgot my Private Gallery PIN.

Tap Forgot PIN and confirm with your fingerprint, Face ID or screen lock to set a new one. You can also change or turn off the PIN in More → Security. After 5 wrong tries there's a 30-second wait.

Is my PIN stored or backed up?

No. The PIN itself is never saved — only a one-way code in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore or iPhone Keychain). It isn't part of your backup.

Are locked photos backed up?

Yes. Locked photos are included in your Google Drive backup and come back when you restore.

Can I get a photo back into my gallery?

Yes. Tap a photo and choose Download to save it back to your phone's gallery. You can also Share or Remove it. Change the grid to large, medium or small — the app remembers your choice.

Security & privacy

How is my data protected?

Sensitive details — titles, numbers, notes, passwords and folder names — are individually encrypted with AES-256 before they're saved. Photos and PDFs are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't open, and the whole app is behind your phone's lock.

Can I lock the app with Face ID or fingerprint?

Yes. Pocket Locker uses your phone's own Face ID, fingerprint or screen lock — there's no separate app password to forget. In Settings → Security you can choose when it locks again: immediately, after 1, 5 or 10 minutes, or never.

Can Pocket Locker, Apple or Google see my documents?

We can't — we don't run any server that receives your documents. Your data stays on your phone unless you turn on Google Drive backup, which goes to a private app folder in your own Google Drive.

What if I lose my phone?

If you turned on Google Drive backup, install Pocket Locker on your new phone and restore — everything comes back. Without a backup, data stored only on a lost phone can't be recovered by anyone, including us. We recommend turning backup on.

Backup & new phone

What does Google Drive backup save?

Your documents, folders, photos and PDFs, and the key needed to unlock them, saved to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive that only Pocket Locker can use. It's off by default, and you can disconnect it at any time.

How do I move to a new phone?

On your old phone, open Settings → Backup and tap Backup Now. On the new phone, install Pocket Locker, sign in to the same Google account in Settings → Backup, and tap Restore. Close and reopen the app after restoring.

Can I move from Android to iPhone (or iPhone to Android)?

Yes. Google Drive backup works across both. On the old phone, open Settings → Backup and tap Backup Now. On the new phone, install Pocket Locker, sign in to the same Google account in Settings → Backup, and tap Restore — your documents, folders, photos and PDFs come across. Premium doesn't transfer between the App Store and Google Play, so on the new platform you'd use the free plan or subscribe there.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Settings → Delete Vault. This permanently erases every document, attachment, reminder and setting on this phone. It can't be undone. Your Google Drive backup (if any) stays in your Drive until you remove it.

What is Auto Backup?

When it's on, Pocket Locker backs up a few seconds after any change and when you leave the app. Only new photos and PDFs are uploaded, so it's quick. You can also tap Backup Now any time.

What does the number on the Sync button mean?

It's how many changes are waiting to be backed up. The Backup page shows your last backup, for example “11 documents · 5 of 5 photos & PDFs backed up”.

Some files didn't back up — what should I do?

The Backup page lists any file that couldn't be backed up. Temporary Google errors are retried automatically; you can also tap Backup Now once you have a good connection.

Why is Auto Backup asking me to Restore or Backup Now?

Auto Backup never overwrites a bigger backup made on another phone. Choose Restore to bring that backup to this phone, or Backup Now to replace it with this phone's vault.

Does restoring bring back my categories and icons?

Yes. Restore brings back documents, photos, PDFs, folders, categories and icons. Premium isn't part of a backup — it comes from your App Store or Google Play account.

Does deleting things free up space?

Yes. Removing a photo, emptying Trash, deleting a category or using Delete Vault frees the space on your phone, including temporary copies. The next backup also removes those deleted files from your Google Drive.

Free vs Premium

What's included for free?

Up to 15 documents, all 7 categories, folders, the document scanner, auto-fill, expiry reminders, Copy All, Light/Dark mode and Google Drive backup.

What does Premium add?

Unlimited documents, unlimited custom categories, multiple reminders per document plus custom date-and-time reminders, export of your vault as a ZIP file, and priority support. Premium is ₹349.00 / year.

What happens if Premium ends?

Nothing you've saved is deleted, and everything stays readable. You return to Free limits — if you have more than 15 documents, you can't add new ones until you're under the limit or renew.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by the App Store or Google Play. In the app, go to Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription, or open your phone's subscription settings (iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Android: Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions).

Does Premium renew automatically?

Yes. Premium is a yearly subscription that renews automatically through the App Store or Google Play until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge; you keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are taken by Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them, not by us. iPhone: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the Pocket Locker purchase. Android: open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, choose the purchase and request a refund. The final decision is Apple's or Google's.

I was charged but the payment failed or is pending.

If a payment is pending (common with UPI), wait a few minutes, then open Settings → Subscription in the app — it checks again automatically. If a renewal payment fails, Apple or Google will retry; update your payment method in your phone's subscription settings.

I got a new phone — do I have to pay again?

No. On a new phone with the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android), open Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. To bring your documents too, restore your Google Drive backup.

Does Premium move between iPhone and Android?

No. Purchases belong to the store you bought from — Premium bought on the App Store works on iPhones with your Apple ID, and Premium bought on Google Play works on Android phones with your Google account. Your documents can still move between iPhone and Android — see Backup & new phone.

I paid but Premium isn't showing.

Open Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. UPI payments can take a few minutes to confirm.

What is Download Reports?

A Premium feature in More → Download Reports. Choose categories and get a ZIP file with every document's details plus its photos and PDFs, ready to share or keep.

How do I redeem a promo code?

Go to More → Subscription and tap promo code. On Android it's redeemed in Google Play; on iPhone Apple's redeem screen opens in the app.

Do restored Trash items count towards the 15-document limit?

Yes. On the Free plan, restoring a document from Trash counts towards your 15 documents.

Is Private Gallery free?

Private Gallery is part of Premium, along with unlimited documents, custom categories, custom date and time reminders, Download Reports and priority support. Share to Pocket Locker, PDF auto-fill, My Files and Show large are free for everyone.

Availability

Is Pocket Locker available in my country?

Right now Pocket Locker is available in India only, on Google Play and the App Store. We're working on launching in more countries soon. Follow us on Instagram or YouTube for launch news.

I'm outside India — can I still use it?

Not yet. Pocket Locker can only be downloaded from the Indian Google Play and App Store for now. When it launches in your country, it'll appear in your store. If you'd like us to come to your country sooner, email support@pocketlocker.app.

Is Pocket Locker on Android?

Yes. Pocket Locker is available on both Android and iPhone. Get it on Google Play or the App Store.

Are the features the same on iPhone and Android?

Yes, the features are the same. A few things work slightly differently: the scanner uses Google's on-device scanner on Android and Apple's on iPhone; copied passwords are cleared after 60 seconds on Android only; on Android 12 and later, allow “Alarms & reminders” so reminders arrive exactly at 9:00 AM; and to share into the app you tap Share › Pocket Locker on Android or Save to Pocket Locker on iPhone.

Does Pocket Locker work without internet?

Yes. You can view, add and search everything offline. Internet is only needed for Google Drive backup and subscriptions.
