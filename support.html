Getting started

What is Pocket Locker?

A private vault for your passwords, IDs, insurance, warranties, and other important documents. Everything is encrypted and stored on your device — nothing is sent to any server unless you choose to enable Google Drive backup yourself.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. Pocket Locker works fully offline with no sign-up required. You only connect a Google account if you choose to turn on Drive backup.

What categories are included by default?

Passwords, Notes, Vehicle, Identity, Warranty, Certificates, and Bank. You can add your own custom categories on the Premium plan.

Free vs. Premium

What's included in the Free plan?

Up to 15 documents, all 7 default categories, local reminders, and optional Google Drive backup — at no cost.

What does Premium add?

Unlimited documents, unlimited custom categories, multi-stage reminders (e.g. 30 days and 7 days before something expires, or a custom date and time), and the ability to download a full copy of your data as a report. Premium is ₹349/year.

What happens to my documents if I don't renew Premium?

Nothing already saved is ever deleted. Your account simply returns to Free plan limits — if you have more than 15 documents, you won't be able to add new ones until you're back under the limit or renew, but everything you already saved stays fully readable.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed through the App Store or Google Play, not directly by us. Open Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription inside the app, or manage it directly from your phone's own subscription settings.

Security & privacy

How is my data protected?

Sensitive fields (passwords, account numbers, notes, and more) are individually encrypted with AES-256 before they're ever saved to your device's local storage — not just hidden behind a lock screen.

Can Pocket Locker or Apple/Google see my documents?

No. Your data is stored locally on your device by default. We don't run servers that receive or store your documents, and we have no way to access them.

What happens if I turn on Google Drive backup?

A backup of your data is saved to a private folder in your own Google Drive — one only your account can access. It's off by default, and you can disconnect it at any time from Settings.

Can I lock the app with Face ID or my fingerprint?

Yes. Pocket Locker uses your device's own biometric lock or screen lock — there's no separate app password to create or forget.

Using the app

I deleted something by mistake — can I get it back?

Yes. Deleted items go to Trash and stay recoverable for 30 days before being permanently removed.

Can I set reminders for things like insurance renewals?

Yes. Any item with a date can have a reminder — choose a lead time like 7 or 30 days before, or (on Premium) a custom date and time, with the option to set more than one reminder per item.

Can I attach a photo or PDF to a document?

Yes. Scan with your camera or attach an existing photo or PDF so the record and the actual document live together.

I'm switching to a new phone — how do I move my data?

Turn on Google Drive backup on your old phone before switching, then restore from that backup when setting up Pocket Locker on your new phone.

Availability

Is Pocket Locker available on Android?

Not yet publicly — it's currently in testing and coming soon to Google Play. It's available now on the App Store for iOS.
