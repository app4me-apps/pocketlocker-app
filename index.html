New · Scanner & Auto-fill FREE

Scan it once. Auto-fill does the typing.

Point your camera at an RC, policy or ID. The edges are found and straightened automatically, and Pocket Locker reads the numbers, names and dates for you to confirm.

  • Automatic edge detection and clean-up
  • Several pages become one PDF
  • Auto-fill numbers, names and dates — you check each one
  • Crop any photo before saving

Organised

A folder for every car, bank and family member.

Ready-made categories — Passwords, Notes, Vehicle, Identity, Warranty, Certificates, Bank — each with a form made for it. Create folders inside, or add your own categories.

  • Bank asks for IFSC and UPI ID; Passwords for username and website
  • Search across titles, numbers and notes
  • Copy All: tick the details you need and copy them in one tap
screenshot-2
Never miss a date

Reminded before insurance, PUC or a licence expires.

Set a reminder 30 or 7 days before any expiry date. When you renew, tap Renew — the new date is saved and the old one goes into the document's history.

  • “Expiring soon” on the home screen
  • Renewal history for every document
  • Reminders for notes at any date and time
