Scan & Auto-fill FREE

Scan it once. Auto-fill does the typing.

Point your camera at an RC book, policy or ID card. Pocket Locker finds the edges, straightens and cleans the page, then reads the numbers, names and dates for you to check.

  • One page saves as a photo; two or more become one PDF
  • ID cards come out at real size — front and back side by side on one A4 page
  • Auto-fill from Scan, Camera, Gallery — or a PDF like an e-policy
  • Share › Pocket Locker from WhatsApp, Gmail or Files — up to 5 at a time
  • Add photos or PDFs up to 20 MB each, crop to the document, view full screen
🔒 Reading happens on your phone. Your photos are never sent to us.

Never miss a date

Reminded before insurance, PUC or a card expires.

Choose 7 or 30 days before any expiry date and a reminder arrives at 9:00 AM — every day from a week before until it's due. Tap the notification and the document opens.

  • “Expiring soon” on Home and a Reminders tab for the next 30 days
  • Tap Renew — the new date is saved, old dates go into history
  • Clear notifications like “Car insurance · Vehicle” — tap to open it
  • Bank card expiry and “change this password by” reminders
  • A gentle Saturday-evening nudge to save one more document — turn it off any time
  • Custom date & time reminders, including Notes PREMIUM
New · Password Generator FREE

Strong passwords, made on your phone.

Open the Generator tab, or tap the key button beside any Password field. Pick the length and characters — Pocket Locker does the rest.

  • 6 to 40 characters: upper, lower, numbers and symbols
  • Avoid look-alike characters like 0 / O and 1 / l
  • Strength meter, plus an encrypted history of the last 50
  • Show large: big numbered boxes in groups of 4, digits and symbols coloured
  • On Android, a copied password is cleared from the clipboard after 60 seconds

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account or internet to use Pocket Locker?

No. There's no sign-up and the app works fully offline. Internet is only used if you turn on backup to your own Google Drive.

Where is my data stored? Can you see it?

Everything stays in the app's private storage on your phone, with titles, numbers, notes and passwords encrypted using AES-256. Pocket Locker has no servers of its own, so we can't see your data.

What happens if I lose or change my phone?

If backup is on, install Pocket Locker on the new phone, connect the same Google Drive and tap Restore. Documents, photos, PDFs, folders, categories and icons come back — even when moving between Android and iPhone.

When do expiry reminders arrive?

At 9:00 AM. With “7 days before” you're reminded every day from a week before until the expiry day. “30 days before” adds one reminder a month ahead. Premium also lets you set a reminder for any date and time.

Is auto-fill safe? Are my photos uploaded?

Auto-fill reads the text on your phone. Images are never sent to us, and you check every value before it's filled in.

Is Pocket Locker available in my country?

Right now Pocket Locker is available in India, on Google Play and the App Store. We're working on bringing it to more countries soon — follow us on Instagram or YouTube for launch news.

What is Private Gallery?

A Premium feature that moves private photos out of your phone's gallery into albums inside Pocket Locker, with an optional 4-digit PIN on top of your phone's lock. Locked photos stay hidden from search and recent lists.

Are the copies I store official documents?

No. Pocket Locker keeps your own copies organised for reference. Stored copies are not official documents.
