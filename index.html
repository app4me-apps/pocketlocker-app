Keep your data secure and access it easily through Pocket Locker, anytime — even with no internet at all.

01

Every piece of data, encrypted

Titles, notes, account numbers, and passwords are individually encrypted with AES-256 before they're ever saved — not just locked behind a password screen.

02

Locked behind your device itself

Face ID, fingerprint, or your phone's own screen lock — there's no separate app password to create or forget. Passwords stay hidden until you tap to show them.

03

Fully offline — no internet required

Every document, password, and photo is stored and readable entirely on your device. You don't need a connection to view, add, or search anything.

04

Restore everything, on any phone

Back up your categories, documents, and every photo or PDF to your own Google Drive — then restore all of it on a new phone in minutes, ready to use offline again immediately.

05

Reminders that don't let you forget

Get reminded before insurance, PUC, licences or warranties expire, renew with one tap, and keep a full history of every past renewal alongside the current details.

06

Deleted by mistake? Still recoverable

Anything you delete sits safely in Trash for 30 days before it's gone for good — plenty of time to undo an accidental delete.

07

Forms made for each category

Bank asks for IFSC and UPI ID, Passwords for username and website, Notes stay simple — and you can rename any field to suit your document.

08

As many categories as you need

Start with ready-made categories, then add your own and customise them however makes sense to you — for genuinely easy access, not a rigid structure.

09

View or download, whenever you want

Every document and photo stays viewable in the app anytime — and Premium lets you export your data as a downloadable copy whenever you need one.
