Your file manager

Documents

Every item lives in a category and a folder, so a car's papers or a family member's IDs stay together — and the phone does most of the typing.

9 built-in categories

NEW

Password, Notes, Vehicle, Identity, Certificate, Bank, Payment, Wealth and Warranty — each with its own form and 3D icon.

Real life: Open the app for the first time and start saving your ID card, car papers or EMIs straight away.

Your own categories

NEW

Add New › choose the kind: Details (any name and icon), Cards, Gallery or Health. Inside Payment and Wealth you can add your own payment categories and investment types too.

Real life: Create a “Property” category for sale deeds and tax receipts, or a “Pets” category for vet records.

Folders

A folder inside a category, e.g. Vehicle › My Car, Identity › Mom, Health › Dad.

Real life: Keep your car's registration, insurance and service bills together in one “Swift” folder.

Move to folder

Move a document to another folder of the same category, or straight into a new folder.

Real life: Sold your old car? Move its papers into an “Old vehicles” folder without re-typing anything.

Forms for your country

NEW

Each category has its own form — e.g. IFSC and UPI ID in India, Sort Code, IBAN, BSB or Routing Number elsewhere. Required fields are marked * and say “Required”.

Real life: Only the fields that make sense for you — no empty boxes to skip.

Tags – linked entries

NEW

“Tags” on the Add / Edit form of every category: tag a card to its bank, a prescription to its doctor and lab report, a policy to its vehicle. Both entries show it under “Tagged”; payments that pay for something show under “Used for”.

Real life: Open your car and see its insurance payment, loan and service bills one tap away.

Important documents checklist

NEW

A small ring on Home (e.g. 5/15) opens the 15 documents everyone in your country should keep — passport, driving licence, insurance, degree, bank details and national IDs. Ticked automatically when you save one.

Real life: See at a glance which important documents your family still hasn't saved.

Quick Fill Types

One tap fills the title — RC Book or PUC in India, V5C Logbook or MOT in the UK, Rego in Australia, Istimara or Fahas in Saudi Arabia — and you can add your own.

Real life: Save five family members' ID cards with the same clean name in seconds.

Rename fields

Tap the pencil on the first three fields to rename them for that one entry.

Real life: Change “Title” to “Policy Name” so a health policy reads exactly like the paper.

Icons for anything

NEW

An emoji, a brand icon or your own photo for any document, folder or category — on the Payment and Wealth lists too. Star a photo icon to reuse it everywhere.

Real life: Put your car's photo on its folder and your bank's logo on its account so you spot them instantly.

Star important documents

Star a document and it stays at the top of its lists.

Real life: Keep your driving licence and passport starred — they're always first when you need them.

Attachments up to 20 MB

Photos, scans and PDFs on any document; crop them and view them full screen.

Real life: Keep the policy PDF together with its number and expiry date, so the original is always with you.

Scan Document

Finds the page edges, straightens and cleans each page. Two or more pages become one PDF.

Real life: Scan a 6-page rental agreement into one neat PDF — no scanner or shop visit needed.

ID card copy

Both sides of an ID card side by side at real card size on one A4 page, like a photocopy.

Real life: Need an ID copy for a bank or KYC form? Share or print the ready-made page.

Auto-fill from photo or PDF

Reads names, numbers and dates from a photo, scan or PDF and fills the form. You check every value. All on the phone.

Real life: Your new car insurance arrives by email — open the PDF and the policy details fill themselves.

Share to Pocket Locker

From Gallery, Files, WhatsApp or email, tap Share › Pocket Locker (iPhone: Save to Pocket Locker) — up to 5 files at once.

Real life: Your landlord sends the rent agreement on WhatsApp — save it to the vault in two taps.

Clean entry page

NEW

Every entry opens with a top card showing what matters most — days left, amount due or value and profit — and the next step: Renew, Mark as Paid, Update value. Expired shows in red, within 7 days in amber.

Real life: Open your insurance and see “Expired 116 days ago” in red — nothing expired goes unnoticed.

Notes

Full-page notes with checklists, lists, formatting and highlights; New Note and My Notes from a long-press on the app icon.

Real life: Write down the locker combination or a packing list privately.

Your payment tracker

Payments

Subscriptions, EMIs and loans, rent, bills, insurance premiums, school fees and monthly investments — what you paid, what's due and what's left. Pocket Locker keeps the record; it never moves money.

Category first

NEW

Choose Subscriptions, Loans / EMI, Rent, Bills, Insurance, Credit Cards, School Fees, Investments & Savings — or your own — and the form changes to fit.

Real life: Adding rent asks for the landlord and due date; adding a loan asks for EMI and tenure.

Loan summary

NEW

From the loan amount, EMI, tenure and start date it works out the interest rate, EMIs paid and pending with a progress bar, balance, total interest, still to pay and the last EMI.

Real life: See you have 34 EMIs and ₹1,58,100 left on your personal loan — without asking the bank.

Mark as Paid / Skip

NEW

One sheet: Paid (shows the next due date) or Skip this time. Paid shows “Paid ✓ 7 Nov” in green until next month.

Real life: No doubt whether this month's rent is done.

Auto-pay or I pay myself

NEW

Auto-pay is recorded as paid by itself after the due date. I pay myself shows “Overdue · Paid?” until you mark it.

Real life: Matches how each payment really works — auto-debit EMIs and manual school fees side by side.

Plans with a length

NEW

Subscriptions, insurance and bills: start date, Ends (Never, 1 month… any number of years), Valid till worked out for you, and how often you pay — a yearly plan can be paid monthly.

Real life: A 1-year policy paid monthly shows “₹42,720 for 1 year · ₹3,560 × 12”.

Renew with Plan History

NEW

When a plan ends, Renew starts the new one with its own dates, amount, policy number and the new policy attached. Each earlier plan becomes one line in Plan History with all its details and documents.

Real life: Last year's and this year's insurance never get mixed up — and last year's policy PDF is still there.

Started on

NEW

An optional first-payment date: every payment since then counts as paid, and Next due becomes the next one.

Real life: Add a loan you started 2 years ago and its full history appears at once.

Change amount

NEW

Rent went from 16,000 to 18,000? Change amount from a date — later payments use the new amount.

Real life: Real amounts in your history and reports.

Dates that stay right

NEW

A payment due on the 31st keeps its day (31 Jan → 28 Feb → 31 Mar). Insurance has a grace period: “Grace · N days left” instead of Overdue.

Real life: No drifting dates, no false “Overdue”.

Linked to accounts and investments

NEW

Pick a saved bank account or card for each payment; SIP, RD and gold-scheme payments add to their Wealth entry when marked Paid.

Real life: Your SIP payment and your mutual fund value stay in sync.

Payment page

NEW

This month or any month or year: Paid and To Pay, a donut by category, Month Total / Paid / Pending, filters, one box per category, a Loans box and search. Paid till, Ended and Expired shown in colour.

Real life: See where the money went this month and what's still to pay.

Reports

NEW

Monthly and yearly payment reports.

Real life: Know exactly what you spent on subscriptions this year.

Payment reminders

NEW

“EMI of ₹10,461 due in 3 days”, “Rent of ₹18,000 due today”, “Netflix is valid till 9 Nov – renew before it ends”, and an 8:00 PM “was it paid?” on the day.

Real life: Never miss an EMI, a bill or a renewal.

Sold, Matured or Closed

Wealth page and Report

Holdings value, Invested and Profit; a donut of where your money is; a box per type; Closed tab; Report with income, realised profit per year and what matures in 90 days.

Maturity reminders

NEW

Your password manager

Passwords & 2FA

A private password book that fills your logins, keeps your 2FA codes and tells you which passwords to fix — all on the phone.

Login entries

Account, username or email, website, password and PIN — all encrypted.

Real life: Keep your Netflix, Wi-Fi, email and utility logins in one place instead of a notebook.

Password Autofill

Fills saved logins in other apps and websites — above the keyboard on Android (Chrome too) and iPhone. Only logins that fit that app or site; one fingerprint or Face ID covers a whole sign-in, and the list gets out of the way once you start typing.

Real life: Open the Instagram app, tap the login box and your saved login is right there.

Strong password on sign-up

“Use a strong password” shows one first (length 8–40), fills it and offers to save the login.

Real life: Creating a new shopping account? Get a strong password that's saved automatically.

Save and update logins

On Android, after you sign in or sign up, Pocket Locker offers to save the login — or update the password of one you already have.

Real life: Changed your email password on the website? Tap Update and the vault has the new one.

Authenticator (2FA codes)

Add a 2FA key by QR code, screenshot or typing; live 6-digit codes with a countdown, tap to copy, all codes on one page with search — fully offline.

Real life: Log in to your email and copy the 2FA code from the same app that holds the password.

Show or remove a 2FA key

After fingerprint or Face ID, see the key to add it to another app; removing a key also asks for fingerprint or Face ID.

Real life: Never locked out of an account.

Password Health

Weak, reused and year-old passwords with a score out of 100; a weak login shows “Weak password · Update”.

Real life: See that two accounts share a password — and change one first.

Password generator

Password (length and character types), Passphrase (3–10 easy words) or Username (random words or an email alias).

Real life: Need a Wi-Fi password guests can type? Generate “Toast-Turtle4-Cake-Wolf”.

Password history per login

The last 5 passwords of every login with dates — copy one or restore it with fingerprint or Face ID.

Real life: Saved the wrong new password by mistake? Restore the old one in a tap.

Show large & copy fields

Big numbered boxes for typing a password on a TV or laptop; copy several fields together; generated passwords kept in an encrypted history.

Real life: Type your Wi-Fi password on the smart TV without mistakes.

Private by design

Security & privacy

The vault is fully offline: no account, no servers, no ads, nothing uploaded unless you choose Google Drive backup.

App lock

Fingerprint, Face ID or your phone's screen lock; Unlock always responds, also right after an update.

Real life: Hand your phone to a child to play a game — your vault stays locked.

Auto-lock

Locks again immediately, after 1, 5 or 10 minutes in the background, or never.

Real life: Leave your phone on the desk and the vault locks itself.

Encryption

Titles, notes, numbers, passwords, 2FA keys and plan and health details are AES-encrypted on the phone; the key is in your phone's secure storage.

Real life: Your data can't be read outside the app.

Private storage

Photos and PDFs live in the app's own storage, which your gallery and other apps can't open.

Real life: Your ID scan never shows up in your gallery.

Safe autofill

Nothing is filled or shown before fingerprint or Face ID; the autofill copy stays on the phone.

Real life: Convenience without giving up security.

No tracking, no ads

No device or advertising IDs and no analytics on your documents.

Real life: Your data stays yours.

Never lose your vault

Backup & restore

A new phone, a lost phone or a reinstall never means losing your documents, payments, wealth or health records.

Google Drive backup

Backs up to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive; only new files are uploaded.

Real life: Your backup lives in your own Google account — not on anyone else's server.

Auto Backup

Backs up by itself a few seconds after a change; the Sync button shows pending changes.

Real life: Save something new and forget about it — it's already backed up.

Backup PIN

An optional 6-digit PIN that locks your Drive backup; restoring needs it.

Real life: Even someone in your Google account can't restore your vault.

Restore — Android ↔ iPhone

NEW

Connect the same Google account and tap Restore; everything comes back with a % progress. Older backups get the new categories, plan lengths and Cards automatically.

Real life: Switch phones and bring everything with you.

Premium kept after restore

Restoring keeps this phone's Premium.

Real life: No surprise “Free” after a restore.

Trash and clean-up

Deleted documents stay 30 days; Select or Select all to restore or delete; removing photos or emptying Trash frees space on the phone and on Drive.

Real life: Undo mistakes, no wasted storage.

Delete Vault

Erases everything on this phone after fingerprint and typing DELETE; the Drive backup stays until deleted there.

Real life: Selling your phone? Wipe the vault first.
