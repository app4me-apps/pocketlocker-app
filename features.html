Your file manager

File Vault

Keep every important document — IDs, vehicle papers, insurance, warranties and certificates — organised in folders and ready in one tap.

7 ready-made categories

Password, Notes, Vehicle, Identity, Warranty, Certificate and Bank — each with a form made for it.

Real life: Open the app for the first time and start saving your ID card or vehicle papers straight away, without setting anything up.

Folders inside categories

Create a folder for each car, bank or family member, like Vehicle › My Car or Identity › Mom.

Real life: Keep your car's registration, insurance and service bills together in one “Swift” folder.

Move to folder

NEW

Move a document to another folder of the same category, or straight into a new folder.

Real life: Sold your old car? Move its papers into an “Old vehicles” folder without re-typing anything.

Custom categories

Make your own category with any name and icon, like Medical, Property or Pets.

Real life: Create a “Medical” category for each family member's prescriptions, reports and health insurance card.

5 entry types

Details, Login, Note, Bank Account and Gallery — each with the right fields, matched to your country.

Real life: A Bank entry asks for IFSC and UPI ID in India, Sort Code in the UK or IBAN in the UAE — no empty fields to skip.

Quick Fill Types

One tap fills the title — RC Book or PUC in India, V5C Logbook or MOT in the UK, Rego in Australia — and you can add your own.

Real life: Save five family members' ID cards with the same clean name in seconds.

Rename fields

Tap the pencil on the first three fields to rename them for that one entry.

Real life: Change “Title” to “Policy Name” so a health policy reads exactly like the paper.

Icons for anything

Use an emoji, a brand icon or your own photo for any document, folder or category.

Real life: Put your car's photo on its folder and your bank's logo on its account so you spot them instantly.

Favourite photo icons

NEW

Star a photo icon and it appears in every icon picker — one tap to reuse it.

Real life: Use the same family photo for your mum's ID, insurance and bank folders without picking it again.

Star important documents

Star a document and it stays at the top of its lists.

Real life: Keep your driving licence and passport starred — they're always first when you need them.

Attachments up to 20 MB

Add photos, scans and PDFs to any document, crop them and view them full screen.

Real life: Keep the policy PDF together with its number and expiry date, so the original is always with you.

Scan Document

Finds the page edges, straightens and cleans each page. Two or more pages become one PDF.

Real life: Scan a 6-page rental agreement into one neat PDF — no scanner or shop visit needed.

ID card copy

Both sides of an ID card placed side by side at real card size on one A4 page, like a photocopy.

Real life: Need an ID copy for a bank or KYC form? Share or print the ready-made front-and-back page.

Auto-fill from photo

Reads names, numbers and dates from a photo or scan and fills the form. You check every value.

Real life: Snap your insurance card and the long policy number and expiry date are filled in for you.

PDF auto-fill

Auto-fill also reads PDFs, like an e-policy or e-ticket.

Real life: Your new car insurance arrives by email — open the PDF and the policy details fill themselves.

Share to Pocket Locker

From Gallery, Files, WhatsApp or email, tap Share › Pocket Locker (iPhone: Save to Pocket Locker) — up to 5 files at once.

Real life: Your landlord sends the rent agreement on WhatsApp — save it to the vault in two taps, right from the chat.

My Files

Every photo and PDF you've saved in one grid. Filter by Photos, PDFs or category and search by document name.

Real life: You remember the warranty bill photo but not where you saved it — find it in one grid.

PDF viewer

PDFs open inside the app with pinch-to-zoom. On Android, password-locked PDFs open in your PDF app.

Real life: Zoom into the fine print of your loan agreement without leaving the vault.

Search

Full-page search with best matches first — titles, providers, numbers, notes, categories and folders. Passwords are never searched.

Real life: Type the name of your bank and see your account, credit card and loan papers together.

All Documents, sorted and filtered

Sort by recently added, expiry date, title or amount; filter by expiring in 30 days, expired or no expiry date.

Real life: Sort by expiry date at the start of the month to see what needs renewing next.

Recent Documents & Expiring Soon

Home shows your latest 5 documents and the nearest expiry with a red or amber “5 days left” label.

Real life: Open the app and see at once that your bike insurance ends next week.

Cleaner Home

NEW

No upgrade box on Home — just a small Free badge. A friendly note appears only when you're near the free limit.

Real life: The app feels calm and free to use; you only hear about limits when they actually matter.

Copy fields & Copy All

Tap any value to copy it, or tick several details and copy them together with their labels.

Real life: Send your account number and branch details to a relative in one WhatsApp message.

Your password manager

Password Vault

Logins, 2FA codes and bank details in one encrypted place — filled for you in other apps, with tools that tell you which passwords to fix.

Login entries

Account name, username or email, website, password and PIN — all encrypted on your phone.

Real life: Keep your Netflix, Wi-Fi, email and utility logins in one place instead of a notebook.

Bank account entries

Bank name, account holder and number, branch details for your country, card number and expiry, netbanking ID.

Real life: Fill a salary or refund form in seconds without hunting for your passbook.

Password Autofill

NEW

Fills saved logins in other apps and websites — above the keyboard on Android (Chrome too) and iPhone. Only logins that match the app or site are offered, after one fingerprint or Face ID.

Real life: Open the Instagram app, tap the login box and your saved login is right there — no copying between apps.

Strong password on sign-up

NEW

On a sign-up or change-password page, “Use a strong password” shows one first (length 8–40, your choice of characters), fills it and offers to save the login.

Real life: Creating a new shopping account? Get a strong password that's saved automatically and never lost.

Save and update logins

NEW

On Android, after you sign in or sign up, Pocket Locker asks to save the login — or to update the password of one you already have.

Real life: Changed your email password on the website? Tap Update and the vault has the new one.

Save a new login from autofill

NEW

On Android, a “Save a new login” row with the Pocket Locker logo appears in the autofill list, with the name, username and website already filled.

Real life: An app that never asks to save passwords? Save the login from the autofill list anyway.

Authenticator (2FA codes)

NEW

Add a 2FA key by scanning the QR code, choosing a screenshot or typing the key. Live 6-digit codes with a countdown, tap to copy, all codes on one page with search — fully offline.

Real life: Log in to your email and copy the 2FA code from the same app that holds the password.

Show or remove a 2FA key

NEW

After fingerprint or Face ID, see the key itself to add it to another app; removing a key also asks for fingerprint or Face ID.

Real life: Moving to a new authenticator? Copy the key safely — and never lose access to the account.

Password Health

NEW

Checks every login for weak, reused (same password on 2+ accounts) and old passwords, with a score out of 100 and the list of what to fix first.

Real life: See that your Facebook and LinkedIn share a password — and change one before it becomes a problem.

Password generator

From the Generator tab or the key button beside a password field: Password (length and character types), Passphrase (3–10 easy words) or Username (random words or an email alias).

Real life: Need a Wi-Fi password guests can type? Generate “Toast-Turtle4-Cake-Wolf” instead of random symbols.

Password history per login

NEW

Every time a login's password changes, the old one is kept. See the last 5 with dates — copy one, or restore it after fingerprint or Face ID.

Real life: Saved the wrong new password by mistake? Restore the old one in a tap.

Generator history

Copied or used generated passwords — including strong passwords from autofill — are kept in an encrypted history.

Real life: Generated a password but closed the website before saving it? Find it in history.

Show large

Shows a password in big numbered boxes in groups of 4, with digits and symbols coloured.

Real life: Type your Wi-Fi password on the smart TV without squinting or making mistakes.

Hidden until you tap

Passwords and PINs stay hidden until you tap the eye to show them.

Real life: Show a colleague a document without exposing the password on the same screen.

Change-password reminders

Add a “change password by” date and get reminded when it's time.

Real life: Get a nudge to change your banking password every few months.

Import & Export

Bring all your passwords from another app, fill dozens of documents from Excel, or take a complete, locked copy of your vault.

Import passwords from other apps

NEW

Import CSV files from Google Password Manager / Chrome, Bitwarden, LastPass, 1Password or Firefox — including 2FA keys when the file has them.

Real life: Switch from Google Password Manager to Pocket Locker in a few minutes, without re-typing a single login.

Excel template

NEW

One Excel file with a sheet for each of your categories, the right columns and an example row, plus 5 numbered steps in the app.

Real life: Type your family's 40 insurance policies on a laptop, then import them all at once.

Import from Excel or CSV

NEW

Reads the template, an export or a CSV; makes new folders, understands dates, and works with files made in another country.

Real life: Moving from a spreadsheet you've kept for years? Import it instead of starting again.

Preview first

NEW

Shows how many entries go into each category and every row that can't be imported, with the reason. Nothing is saved until you tap Import.

Real life: Spot a wrong column before it creates 50 messy entries.

Export as a locked ZIP

NEW

One ZIP locked with a password you choose (AES-256), after fingerprint or Face ID: an Excel file with every detail plus all photos and PDFs in folders. Opens with 7-Zip, WinRAR, Keka or The Unarchiver.

Real life: Keep a complete, safe copy of the vault on your laptop — or hand your parents' documents to a lawyer in one file.

Your photo manager

Photo Vault

Move private photos out of your phone's gallery into albums inside the locked vault, with an extra PIN on top.

Private Gallery category

Create a category with the 🔒 Gallery type and add albums like Family or Private.

Real life: Keep family photos separate from the hundreds of screenshots in your phone's gallery.

Lock photos

Pick up to 30 photos at a time, or Share › Pocket Locker from any app. No form to fill.

Real life: Select last month's private photos in your gallery and lock them all at once.

Remove from gallery

After locking, remove the photos from your phone's gallery. Android deletes them for good; iPhone keeps them 30 days in Recently Deleted. The app tells you if it can't.

Real life: Hand your phone to a friend to scroll your gallery without worrying about what they'll see.

Private by default

Locked photos don't appear in Recent Documents, Expiring Soon, All Documents, My Files or Search.

Real life: Show someone your insurance document without a private photo popping up next to it.

Private Gallery PIN

One 4-digit PIN for all locked galleries, asked even when the app is already open. A blue lock marks the category.

Real life: Even if your phone is unlocked and the app is open, your private album still needs the PIN.

PIN safety

5 wrong tries means a 30-second wait; it locks again when you leave or after 15 seconds away. Reset or change it in More › App Lock & PINs with fingerprint, Face ID or screen lock.

Real life: A curious child can't guess their way in — and you can reset a forgotten PIN with your fingerprint.

Photo sizes

Browse in large (2 per row), medium (3) or small (5) — your choice is remembered.

Real life: Switch to small to scroll through a big album quickly.

Download, share or remove

Tap a photo to download it back to your gallery, share it or remove it.

Real life: Send one photo from the vault to family on WhatsApp without unlocking the whole album.

Included in backup

Locked photos are part of your Google Drive backup. The PIN itself is never stored or backed up.

Real life: Change phones and your private albums come back with everything else.

Custom date & time

A reminder at any date and time, including for Notes — picked with your phone's own calendar and clock.

Clear notifications

The title shows the document and its category, e.g. “Car insurance · Vehicle”. Tap to open it.

Real life: Know exactly what's due from the lock screen, without opening the app.

One-step permissions

NEW

Right after you allow notifications, Android 12+ asks for “Alarms & reminders” too; More › Notifications shows a clear card if a step is missing.

Real life: No hidden second switch — your 9 AM reminder actually arrives at 9 AM.

Reminders tab

Everything due in the next 30 days, what's expired and what's later — in one clean list.

Real life: Plan this month's renewals in one look.

Renew with history

Enter the new date — and the amount paid, in your currency — when you renew; old dates are kept as history.

Real life: See what you paid for car insurance last year before you renew this year.

Card expiry reminders

Get reminded before a debit or credit card expires.

Real life: Update your card on subscriptions before the old one stops working.

Saturday reminder

Saturday 8:00 PM: a gentle nudge to spend two minutes saving one more document. Turn it off in More › Notifications.

Real life: Build your vault little by little — one document every weekend.

Private by design

Security & privacy

No account, no servers of our own, no ads and nothing uploaded unless you choose Google Drive backup.

App lock

Opens with fingerprint, Face ID or your phone's screen lock — set up in More › App Lock & PINs.

Real life: Hand your phone to a child to play a game — your vault stays locked.

Auto-lock

Locks again immediately, after 1, 5 or 10 minutes in the background, or never.

Real life: Leave your phone on the desk and the vault locks itself.

AES-256 encryption

Titles, notes, numbers, passwords and 2FA keys are encrypted on your phone; the key is in your phone's secure storage.

Real life: Your data can't be read outside the app, even if someone copies the phone's files.

Private storage

Photos and PDFs live in the app's own storage, which your gallery and other apps can't open.

Real life: Your ID scan never shows up in your gallery or WhatsApp's photo picker.

Safe autofill

NEW

Nothing is filled or shown before fingerprint or Face ID; the autofill copy of your logins stays on the phone and is deleted when Autofill is turned off.

Real life: Convenience of autofill without anyone else being able to fill your passwords.

Works fully offline

View, add and search everything with no internet — even 2FA codes.

Real life: Find your passport number or a 2FA code on a flight with no signal.

No tracking, no ads

No device or advertising IDs and no analytics on your documents.

Real life: Your documents stay yours — never used to show you ads.

Never lose your vault

Backup & restore

A new phone, a lost phone or a reinstall doesn't mean losing your documents, passwords or 2FA codes.

Google Drive backup

Backs up to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only new photos and files are uploaded. The Backup page shows its status at the top.

Real life: Your backup lives in your own Google account — not on anyone else's server.

Auto Backup

Backs up a few seconds after you add, change or delete something. The Sync button shows changes waiting.

Real life: Save a new document and forget about it — it's already backed up.

Backup PIN

NEW

An optional 6-digit PIN that locks your Drive backup. Restoring on a new phone needs it; change it any time with fingerprint or Face ID.

Real life: Even if someone gets into your Google account, they can't restore your vault.

Restore — Android ↔ iPhone

Connect the same Google account on a new phone, tap Restore and enter your backup PIN if set — documents, passwords, 2FA codes and photos come back.

Real life: Switch from Android to iPhone and bring everything with you.

Restore progress

NEW

Shows “Restoring from Google Drive… 45%” up to 100%, then how many documents, photos and PDFs came back. Files download 4 at a time.

Real life: See the restore working on your new phone instead of wondering if it's stuck.

Premium kept after restore

NEW

Restoring a backup keeps this phone's Premium — it never turns into Free because of a restore.

Real life: Restore on a new phone and keep using every Premium feature straight away.

Disconnect Google Drive

NEW

Ends Pocket Locker's access to your Google account and turns Auto Backup off; the backup on Drive is kept.

Real life: Changing Google accounts? Disconnect cleanly and connect the new one.

Backup report

Shows the last backup time, documents and files backed up, and anything that failed.

Real life: Check that yesterday's backup worked before you reset your phone.

30-day Trash

Deleted documents stay 30 days. Select or Select all to restore or delete many at once.

Real life: Deleted the wrong policy? Restore it from Trash.

Storage clean-up

Removing photos, emptying Trash or deleting a category frees your phone's space; the next backup removes them from Drive too.

Real life: Clear out old scans and actually get the space back.

Delete Vault

Erases everything on this phone after fingerprint and typing DELETE. Your Drive backup stays until you delete it there.

Real life: Selling your phone? Wipe the vault completely first.
